Quizoo ACCOUNT DELETION
🦉💣 🗑️🧨

Delete your account

Game over, by choice. Two ways to erase your Quizoo save — instantly inside the app, or by a request you can send from right here.

⚡ In-app: immediate ✉️ Web request: ≤ 30 days
⚡

Fastest way — inside the app

01
  1. Open Quizoo → Settings
  2. Tap Delete account
  3. Confirm by typing your username — this is the safety net, so a confused tap can't nuke the save

That's it: your whole save and every profile field is erased permanently, with no waiting period.

⚠️ Cancelling a Quizoo Pro subscription is not this button! Subscriptions live on your App Store / Google Play account — cancel there first, then delete (Terms §6).

✉️

Request deletion from this page

02

If you can't open the app (lost device, uninstalled, account stuck) — send us a deletion request by e-mail. Use the button; it fills in everything we need for you:

🗑️  Send a deletion request

If the button won't open your mail app, write to octabytes.studio@gmail.com with the subject Quizoo account deletion request, or open an issue at our public repo.

What to include

  • Your username and avatar emoji (as shown on the leaderboards)
  • If you never upgraded: your guest tag (e.g. "Guest 4821")
  • How you saved: Google / Apple / e-mail — or "guest"

🛡️ We may ask one confirming question before deleting, so nobody can wipe somebody else's save with a forged request. If your mail is the one you used for saving, that's identity enough — no further questions.

⏱️ We aim to complete requests within a week, and always within 30 days (with the Privacy Policy's §11 promise). You'll get a confirmation reply when it's done.

🧹

What disappears & what doesn't

03

Deleted permanently

  • Profile — username, avatar, country/city, language preference and timestamps
  • All progression — XP, player level, coins, gems, hearts, streaks and streak freezes, stars, badges
  • Competition data — ELO, league standing, leaderboard entries, duel history and match results
  • Your Pro status flag and its device linkage

Not deleted, because it isn't ours

  • Store billing records — Apple / Google (and their mediator RevenueCat) keep purchase history for accounting and refund handling under their own policies; we never see your card numbers
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics already merged into usage numbers (Firebase) — no longer tied to a person
  • Provider copies from routine backup cycles, which auto-expire in their normal window

🫥 Guest saves too: e-mail us your guest tag or simply reset the app — dormant guest saves are also cleared through the retention policy (Privacy §13).

🎭

Life after deletion

04

You can always reinstall and start a new save — it'll begin fresh (a new guest identity, 0 XP, empty streak). Your old username, if you had one, is reused by whoever picks it first.

For the full data story, read the Privacy Policy; for the rules of playing (and the deletion terms' fine print), the Terms of Service.
