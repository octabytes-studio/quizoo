The short version

01
  • We only collect what the game needs to save your score and run the features you use.
  • A Quizoo save is created automatically — you don't need an email to start playing, and your initial identity is an anonymous, system-generated guest.
  • You can fill your profile (username, avatar, country, city) — it's optional and you choose what it says.
  • We use trusted third parties: Supabase for the save service, Firebase for analytics and crash reports, Google AdMob for ads and RevenueCat to manage Quizoo Pro purchases.
  • You can delete your account permanently from Settings → Delete account, right inside the app.
  • We never sell your personal data and never sell it to data brokers.

🛟 The details in the numbered sections below are the real policy — this summary is here to make it comfortable, not to replace it.

🦉

About Quizoo & this policy

02

Quizoo ("Quizoo", "the app") is a quiz game for iOS and Android phones — 30-level topic ladders, daily challenges, leagues, realtime 1v1 duels and global leaderboards. Quizoo is an independent ("indie") project maintained by its developer — the same one who owns this page.

When you install the app on your device, the developer of Quizoo acts as the data controller for the personal data described here, while the service partners listed in §16 — Service partners act as processors on our behalf.

This policy applies to the mobile app. The same web build served for the app's public pages collects essentially nothing, and the in-app legal links (paywall and save-progress sheets) open on this policy.

🧩 Platforms like the Apple App Store and Google Play, and sign-in providers like Google and Apple, process data under their own privacy policies. We link to theirs in §16.

📝

Information you give us

03

Your save

The app creates an anonymous guest session the first time you launch it. No signup is required. If you want your progress on the leaderboard and across devices, you upgrade that save with Google, Apple or e-mail — which gives us your e-mail address, display name and profile picture exactly as that provider shares them (Apple's "Hide My E-mail" relay and "Share name only" options are honoured automatically).

Your profile

You can edit the editable fields (Settings → Profile):

  • Username — 3–20 characters, shown on leaderboards and to duel opponents
  • Avatar — an emoji from our included set (like 🐼 or 🦁)
  • Country & city — used for a home flag on leaderboards and keeping local topics sensible

🎭 Pick a username you're happy to be publicly attached to (see §9). Don't put your address, phone number, or anything you'd regret into it.

Support during play

If you reach out to us via the public repository (see §18), we receive whatever you type into that post.

🎮

Gameplay & technical data

04

Playing the game generates the numbers that make it a game. We store them so your save survives between sessions and devices:

  • Progress — XP, player level, coins, gems, hearts, streak days and streak freezes, level stars, and badge completions
  • Skill & leagues — result per question and level, scores, the anti-repeat count of which questions you've seen, ELO rating, league placement
  • Matches — duel rounds against matched opponents in realtime (labelled "practice bot" matches are separate)
  • Session timestamps — when you last played (streak calculations) and when your save was created

On the device itself we also keep settings like chosen language, sound effects, theme mode and your selected content language.

🫥 Guests are displayed as "Guest 4821"-style names until they save — the tag is generated from your save ID and doesn't identify you by e-mail or platform account.

📊

Analytics & crash reports

05

We use the Firebase suite (Google) to make the game better:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — pseudonymous usage events (which features are popular, where players drop off) so we can spot dead screens and difficulty spikes
  • Firebase Crashlytics — automatic crash reports (build version, device model, stack trace) so we can fix what actually broke
  • Remote Config — server-managed feature flags (daily-challenge rewards, ad frequencies, availability of Pro) with which we ship tweaks without re-publishing an app
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver notifications, such as reminders, if you allow them

Analytics identifiers are pseudonymous and aren't used by us to build advertising profiles, and we do not try to re-identify you from that data.

📣

Advertising

06

Quizoo is free because the game shows ads. We use Google AdMob, which may serve banner, native, interstitial or rewarded formats.

AdMob relies on device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID if you haven't disabled it; on iOS, the advertising identifier if you permitted tracking) and on consent set in the app. Depending on that consent, ads may be contextual or personalised.

In regions where consent law applies (EEA/UK etc.), Google's UMP consent form appears before the first ad and can be reached later through your device settings and the "Ad privacy choices" row in Settings → Privacy, right in the app.

Where you exercise the controls (limit ad tracking, personalised-ads opt out, IAB/behavioural choices), those choices govern AdMob's processing — see Google's ad privacy page via §16.

💎

Purchases & Quizoo Pro

07

Quizoo Pro (ad-free plus extra hearts, power-ups, and revive streak-freeze perks) is offered as monthly, yearly and lifetime plans, and purchases are completed by Apple or Google.

  • We never see or store your card numbers — billing happens inside the App Store / Play Store.
  • RevenueCat receives a receipt/transaction reference from the store plus an anonymous app-user ID, so we can grant your Pro entitlement and honour Restore purchases. It never receives your billing details.
  • Your Pro status is stored as a flag on your save (is_pro) and can be re-fetched or re-granted by restoring purchases.

Refunds, cancellation and billing mechanics are covered in our Terms of Service.

🔊

Audio, images & content

08

Some questions include narration, images or figures.

  • Narration is recorded in advance — at content-authoring time — and streamed from our save service's object storage. It's generated with third-party text-to-speech tools (ElevenLabs) from authored question text only.
  • We do not record, request, or use your microphone or voice. Quizoo never asks for microphone permission.
  • Images are pre-selected open-licence artwork hosted in the same storage; licences that need them carry credit inside the question's explanation.
  • Camera, contacts, location and health data are never requested.
👀

What's public on Quizoo

09

Quizoo is competitive by design. The following are visible to other players and on public leaderboards:

  • Your username and avatar (or your generated "Guest" name if you haven't saved)
  • Your country, where leaderboards or duel screens show flags
  • Your gameplay stats that feed leaderboards — XP, league, ELO bracket, wins and duels

🚫 We never publish a player's e-mail, sign-in identity, birthdate, precise location, or contact details. Ever.

Choose your username and avatar accordingly — they're your public face across leaderboards, duels and spectator screens.

Children's privacy

10

Quizoo is not directed at children under 13 (or under the higher minimum age that applies in your jurisdiction, e.g. up to 16 in parts of the EEA). We didn't design it for children and don't knowingly collect their data.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child gave us personal data, write to us via §18 and we'll delete that save and all associated data as promptly as we can.

The ads are rated family-friendly on the stores, but they're not individually child-directed. If you're worried about a child's exposure to ads, see §6.

🗑️

Deleting your data

11

Delete account

Settings → Delete account does it right in the app: type your username to confirm, and your entire save — profile, XP, coins, gems, hearts, badges, streaks, league and duel history — is permanently removed. Deletion is processed promptly; our providers' routine backup cycles mean copies can linger for a short window before those records are fully overwritten.

Guest saves

You're welcome to keep playing as a guest. A guest save that's never upgraded but also never played again doesn't hang around forever — dormant guest saves are cleared through the retention window in §13.

Platform store purchases

Deleting your Quizoo account does not cancel an active Quizoo Pro subscription — cancel that inside the App Store / Google Play account settings first. See Terms §6.

⚠️ Deleting is permanent. Coins, XP, streaks and Pro flags aren't recoverable once deletion finishes, even if you reinstall.

🔐

How we protect data

12
  • All network traffic runs over TLS/HTTPS.
  • Your save lives in a hosted database (Supabase) whose access is governed by row-level security — players can only read or edit the privacy-sensitive fields of their own save, and certain fields (XP, gems, hearts) aren't client-editable at all.
  • Secrets (an API key for narration generation, server-adjacent keys) are kept out of the shipped app and out of the source tree.
  • Payments and platform identity belong to Apple / Google — the strongest identity infrastructure in mobile.

🛡️ No system is perfectly secure on the internet. We apply reasonable measures, but can't guarantee absolute security — see Terms §11.

⏳

Retention

13

We keep the data described here only while it's needed:

  • Active saves — kept while the account exists (or while a guest save remains usable on a device).
  • Deleted accounts — erased promptly per §11; replicas and backups clear within the provider's routine backup cycle.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports and aggregated analytics expire according to the provider's retention (usually 90 days to 14 months).

International transfers

14

Quizoo's save service, analytics, ads and purchase infrastructure operate worldwide, so your data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required (e.g. transfers out of the EEA/UK), we rely on our partners' use of Standard Contractual Clauses or similar mechanisms, which they disclose in their own policies (§16).

Your rights

15

If a law where you live grants these (EU/EEA/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in California, LGPD in Brazil, and so on), you may generally be able to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold (your profile and stats are already visible in the app)
  • Rectify — fix incorrect data (username, country, etc.) in Settings, any time
  • Erase — delete your save (§11), in app, in seconds
  • Object/restrict — opt out of ad personalisation (§6) and notifications
  • Portability — on request, we'll send you a structured file of your core stats
  • Withdraw consent — e.g. the UMP ad-consent flow in regions that require it

💸 We do not sell your personal data, and we never have. Ads are placed by Google AdMob under Google's rules — the only related profiles come from AdMob's own identifiers per §6.

Reach us via §18 and we'll acknowledge or resolve your request (we aim for ≤ 30 days, usually much faster). You can also complain to your local supervisory authority.
